Call Break Scorecard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Call Break Scorecard ("the app") is a scorekeeping app for the card game Call Break. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information or usage data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

Player names and scores you enter are saved only on your device so your game is still there when you reopen the app. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app removes it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
