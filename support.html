How scoring works

If a player wins at least as many hands as they called, they score their call plus 0.1 for each extra hand (call 3, win 4 = 3.1). If they win fewer, they lose their call (call 3, win 2 = −3), or score 0 if you chose the "Score zero" rule.

Fixing a mistake

Tap "Undo Last Round" on the standings screen to re-enter the last round.
